Drop your photo here, named exactly: photo.jpg (or .png)
Full path: public/photo.jpg

Then open components/About.tsx and set:
  const aboutPhoto = "/photo.jpg";

Tips for the photo, to match the site's flat editorial look:
- A simple portrait works better than a busy background
- It renders in grayscale automatically to match the black/cream palette
- Roughly portrait orientation (3:4) crops best into the photo slot
